## Connect your Bluesky account

Site Poster Pro connects with your **handle** and an **app password**. Never use your normal Bluesky password.

<Steps>

1. **Create an app password in Bluesky.**
   In Bluesky, go to **Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords** and create a new one, for example named *Site Poster Pro*. Copy it.

2. **Open Connections** in Site Poster Pro and go to the **Bluesky** section.

3. **Enter your handle**, for example `your-handle.bsky.social`. Leave out the `@`.

4. **Paste the app password.**

5. **Click Connect Bluesky.**
   If you already have an account connected, the button is called **Add another Bluesky account**. You see *Bluesky account connected* and the account appears in the list.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="App password, not your login password">
An app password only gives access to posting and can be revoked in Bluesky at any time without changing your real password. If you revoke it, connect the account again with a new app password.
</Aside>

Publishing for clients? Assign the account to the right client under **Clients** → **+ Connections**. See [Working with clients](/content/working-with-clients/).

## How your TXT files become Bluesky posts

Bluesky posts are short, so Site Poster Pro converts each TXT file into one post:

- The `<h1>` becomes the **headline** of the post.
- The article text is shortened to an **excerpt** that fits Bluesky's text limit.
- The **first link** in the article is kept in the post when possible.

Long articles therefore lose most of their text. For Bluesky, it works best to start from content written as short posts.

## Generate Bluesky posts with WP Publisher Pro

The fastest way to get posts that fit is the social posts generator in [WP Publisher Pro](https://wppublisherpro.com/). This step is separate from Site Poster Pro.

1. In WP Publisher Pro, open **social posts**, enter your keywords and URLs and choose **Bluesky** as the platform. The posts then stay within Bluesky's character limit.
2. Add your own prompt to steer the content.
3. Generate the posts and **download the zip file**. Unzip it: you get one TXT file per post.

<Aside type="tip" title="Put the link in its own post">
Instead of linking to your money site in every post, create **two to four posts per target page** and put the URL in only one of them. The other posts talk about the topic without a link. This looks more natural than a feed full of identical links.
</Aside>

## Publish or schedule your posts

<Steps>

1. **Open TXT Import and select Bluesky** under *Publish to*.

2. **Select the client** (optional). Only the Bluesky accounts assigned to that client are shown.

3. **Upload your TXT files.** Select the unzipped files; TXT Import accepts `.txt` files, not the zip itself.

4. **Choose the Bluesky account.** Just connected one and don't see it? Click **Refresh**.

5. **Add images (optional).**
   Choose **one image** for all posts, or **exactly one image per TXT file**. Use JPG, PNG or WebP of **at most 1 MB**: that is Bluesky's limit.

6. **Choose the post status:**
   - **Publish immediately**: posts go live right away.
   - **Schedule posts**: set the first post date, first post time and the interval between posts.

7. **Click Publish to Bluesky** (or **Schedule to Bluesky**).
   The results show every post with its status and, once published, its URL.

</Steps>

## How scheduling works

Site Poster Pro shows a preview with the first and last publication time and the total number of posts. Minute intervals use steps of 10 minutes, with a minimum of 10. The first post time must be in the future.

Site Poster Pro stores the schedule and publishes each post at its time, so **you can close your browser after scheduling**.

A scheduled post gets its Bluesky URL when it is published. While the page is open, the results update automatically every 30 seconds; you can also click **Refresh URLs**. Afterwards you find all URLs under [Published URLs](/results/published-urls/), and all scheduled and published posts in the [Publishing Calendar](/scheduling/publishing-calendar/).

## Troubleshooting

**The connection fails.**
Check that you used an **app password**, not your normal password, and that the handle is complete (for example `name.bsky.social`) without `@`. Created the app password a while ago? It may have been revoked: create a new one.

**"No Bluesky accounts connected yet."**
Connect an account under Connections first. Already did? Click **Refresh** in the publishing panel.

**My account is not in the list.**
If a client is selected, only accounts assigned to that client are shown. Assign the account under **Clients**, or select *No client / Unassigned*.

**My post is much shorter than my article.**
That is expected. Bluesky posts have a strict character limit, so Site Poster Pro uses the headline and a short excerpt. Generate posts written for Bluesky instead.

**An image is rejected.**
Bluesky accepts images up to 1 MB. Compress the image or save it as JPG or WebP.

**"Choose either 1 image for all posts or exactly N images for N posts."**
The number of images does not match the number of TXT files.

**"Choose a future start date and time for the Bluesky schedule."**
The first post time is in the past. Pick a later date or time.

**A scheduled post has no URL yet.**
That is expected. The URL is created when the post is published at its scheduled time.
