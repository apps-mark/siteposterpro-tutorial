## Before you start

- **A LiveJournal account.** Create it on LiveJournal first. Site Poster Pro cannot create accounts for you.
- **New account? Publish the first post yourself**, directly on LiveJournal, before you connect it to Site Poster Pro.
- Your articles as **TXT files with HTML**. Each file becomes one post: the first `<h1>` becomes the title and the remaining HTML becomes the post. Files without an H1 are blocked before publishing. See [TXT import format](/content/txt-import-format/).
- Optional: images in JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF format.

## Connect your LiveJournal account

<Steps>

1. **Open Connections** in Site Poster Pro and go to the **LiveJournal** section.

2. **Enter your LiveJournal username and password.**

3. **Click Connect LiveJournal.**
   If you already have an account connected, the button is called **Add another LiveJournal account**.

4. **Wait for the confirmation.**
   Connecting to LiveJournal can take **30 to 40 seconds**. That is normal. When it is done you see *LiveJournal account connected* and the account appears in the list.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Use a LiveJournal account that you only use for publishing, with its own strong password. If you change the password on LiveJournal later, connect the account again.
</Aside>

Publishing for clients? Assign the account to the right client under **Clients** → **+ Connections**. See [Working with clients](/content/working-with-clients/).

## Publish or schedule your articles

<Steps>

1. **Open TXT Import and select LiveJournal** under *Publish to*.

2. **Select the client** (optional). Only the LiveJournal accounts assigned to that client are shown.

3. **Upload your TXT files.**

4. **Choose the LiveJournal account.** Just connected one and don't see it? Click **Refresh**.

5. **Add images (optional).**
   Choose **one image** for all posts, or **exactly one image per TXT file**. Images are matched to the articles in order.

6. **Choose the post status:**
   - **Publish immediately**: posts go live right away.
   - **Schedule posts**: set the first post date, first post time and the interval between posts.

7. **Click Publish to LiveJournal** (or **Schedule to LiveJournal**).
   The results list every post with its status. For published posts you get the URL and a **Copy URLs** button.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Start with one test post">
Publish a single article with one image first and check it on LiveJournal. Then upload the rest of your articles.
</Aside>

## How scheduling works

Site Poster Pro shows a preview with the first and last publication time and the total number of posts. Minute intervals use steps of 10 minutes, with a minimum of 10. The first post time must be in the future.

Site Poster Pro stores the schedule and publishes each post at its time, so **you can close your browser after scheduling**. A scheduled post gets its public LiveJournal URL when it is actually published. You then find it under [Published URLs](/results/published-urls/).

### Take it slow on new accounts

LiveJournal is strict with new accounts. If you already published a few posts on a new account today, start the schedule tomorrow and keep a wide interval. In the video, 15 posts start at 9 AM with one post every 5 hours, spread over 4 days.

<Aside type="caution">
This is a practical recommendation, not an official LiveJournal limit.
</Aside>

## Check your posts

- **Publishing Calendar:** select the client to see every scheduled and published LiveJournal post. See [Publishing Calendar](/scheduling/publishing-calendar/).
- **Published URLs:** filter on client and platform **LiveJournal** to see and copy the URLs of all published posts. See [Published URLs](/results/published-urls/).

## Troubleshooting

**Connecting or publishing is slow.**
LiveJournal often responds slowly. Connecting can take 30 to 40 seconds and publishing speed varies. Wait for the result before you click again.

**"No LiveJournal accounts connected yet."**
Connect an account under Connections first. Already did? Click **Refresh** in the publishing panel.

**My account is not in the list.**
If a client is selected, only accounts assigned to that client are shown. Assign the account under **Clients**, or select *No client / Unassigned*.

**The connection fails.**
Check your username and password by logging in on LiveJournal itself. Then try again. Changed your password? Connect the account again.

**"Choose a future start date and time for the LiveJournal schedule."**
The first post time is in the past. Pick a later date or time.

**"Choose either 1 image for all posts or exactly N images for N posts."**
The number of images does not match the number of TXT files.

**A scheduled post has no URL yet.**
That is expected. The URL is created when LiveJournal publishes the post at its scheduled time.

**A warning says results could not be saved to Published URLs or My Calendar.**
The posts were published, but Site Poster Pro could not record them. Copy the URLs from the results with **Copy URLs** before you leave the page.
