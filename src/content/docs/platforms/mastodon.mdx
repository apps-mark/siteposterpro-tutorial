Site Poster Pro publishes posts to Mastodon on any instance, such as mastodon.social. Because every Mastodon instance is hosted independently, you connect with two things from your own instance: the **instance URL** and an **access token**. After that you can publish or schedule as many posts as you want.

## Get your access token in Mastodon

You create a small "application" on your Mastodon instance. It gives you an access token for Site Poster Pro.

<Steps>

1. **Log in to your Mastodon account** and go to **Preferences → Development**.

2. **Click New Application.**

3. **Set up the application.**
   Enter a name, for example *Site Poster Pro*. Leave the redirect URI as it is. Check the **read** and **write** scopes and click **Submit**.

4. **Open the application** by clicking its name in the list.

5. **Copy your access token** from the application page.

6. **Note your instance.**
   Look at your full handle, for example `@yourname@mastodon.social`. The part after the second `@` is your instance: here `mastodon.social`.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep your access token private">
The access token gives control over your Mastodon account. Never share it. If it leaks, delete the application in Preferences → Development: the token stops working right away. Then create a new one and connect again.
</Aside>

## Connect Mastodon to Site Poster Pro

<Steps>

1. **Open Connections** in Site Poster Pro and go to the **Mastodon** section.

2. **Enter your instance as a full URL.**
   If your handle ends in `@mastodon.social`, enter `https://mastodon.social`.

3. **Paste your access token.**

4. **Click Save & Connect.**
   The account appears in the Mastodon list. You can connect more accounts, also on different instances.

</Steps>

Publishing for clients? Assign the account to the right client under **Clients** → **+ Connections**. See [Working with clients](/content/working-with-clients/).

## Generate Mastodon posts with WP Publisher Pro

Mastodon posts have a character limit. If a post is too long, it won't be published. The easiest way to stay within the limit is the social posts generator in [WP Publisher Pro](https://wppublisherpro.com/):

1. In WP Publisher Pro, open **Social Posts** and enter your keywords and URLs.
2. Under *Social Posts*, check **Mastodon**. The posts are then written to fit Mastodon.
3. Generate the posts and **download the zip file**. Unzip it: you get one TXT file per post.

## Publish or schedule your posts

<Steps>

1. **Open TXT Import and select Mastodon** under *Publish to*.

2. **Select the client** (optional). Only the Mastodon accounts assigned to that client are shown.

3. **Upload your TXT files.** Select the unzipped files; TXT Import accepts `.txt` files, not the zip itself.

4. **Choose the Mastodon account.** Just connected one and don't see it? Click **Refresh**.

5. **Choose the post status:**
   - **Publish immediately**: posts go live right away.
   - **Schedule posts**: set the first post date, first post time and the interval between posts.

6. **Click Publish to Mastodon** (or **Schedule to Mastodon**).
   The results show every post with its status and, once published, its URL.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Start with one post">
Publish one post immediately first and check it on Mastodon. Then schedule the rest. In the video, the remaining three posts are scheduled one every 4 hours.
</Aside>

Mastodon posts are text only: images are not added.

## How scheduling works

Site Poster Pro shows a preview with the first and last publication time and the total number of posts. Minute intervals use steps of 10 minutes, with a minimum of 10. The first post time must be in the future.

Site Poster Pro publishes each post at its time, so **you can close your browser after scheduling**. You can check all scheduled and published posts in the [Publishing Calendar](/scheduling/publishing-calendar/): filter on the client and the platform **Mastodon**. The URLs of published posts are under [Published URLs](/results/published-urls/).

## Troubleshooting

**The connection fails.**
Check that the instance URL is complete and starts with `https://`, for example `https://mastodon.social`, and that it is the instance where your account lives. Check that you copied the full access token and that the application has the **read** and **write** scopes.

**A post is not published.**
It is probably longer than Mastodon allows. Shorten it, or generate the posts with Mastodon selected in WP Publisher Pro.

**My account is not in the list.**
If a client is selected, only accounts assigned to that client are shown. Assign the account under **Clients**, or select *No client / Unassigned*.

**It worked before, but now it fails.**
The application may have been deleted on Mastodon, or the token was revoked. Create a new application and access token, and connect again.

**"Choose a future start date and time."**
The first post time is in the past. Pick a later date or time.
