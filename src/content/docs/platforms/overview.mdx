Site Poster Pro publishes to each platform in one of three ways. Every platform guide shows which one applies:

- <span class="sp-badge sp-badge--automated">Fully automated publishing</span> Site Poster Pro publishes through the platform's own connection. After you connect your account once, no further steps are needed.
- <span class="sp-badge sp-badge--extension">Publishes via browser extension</span> Publishing runs in your own browser through the Site Poster browser extension. Your browser must be open while posts are published.
- <span class="sp-badge sp-badge--manual">Requires manual steps</span> Some steps have to be done by you on the platform itself. The guide tells you exactly which ones.

:::note[Being verified]
The publishing method per platform is being verified against the live app. Platform guides are published one by one as each one is checked.
:::

Choose a platform in the sidebar to see how to connect it.
